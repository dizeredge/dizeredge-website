TECHNICAL QUESTIONS

Before you integrate.

Where are the API documentation and sandbox?

Contact DizerEdge to confirm the current API contracts, documentation, SDKs, and sandbox availability for your selected capabilities. Public endpoints and test credentials are not published on this website.

Can we keep our existing identity provider?

The integration approach supports working with existing customer systems. SSO, IAM, session handling, and entitlement responsibilities are defined for your architecture.

Which biometric devices are supported?

Fingerprint and physical access integrations depend on compatible platform frameworks, scanners, and access-control hardware. Confirm your device models and deployment environment during scoping.

Are dedicated deployment options available?

Dedicated or customer-controlled environments can be evaluated where technically and commercially appropriate. Availability, data location, and operational responsibilities require a specific agreement.
