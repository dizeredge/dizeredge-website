To comply with enterprise accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA requirements for financial software and digital platforms), all text and interactive elements must maintain strict contrast ratios against their background canvases. 

Primary body text set in Slate Steel (`#334155`) or Deep Midnight Navy (`#0A192F`) on a Crisp Cloud (`#F8FAFC`) background exceeds a 12:1 contrast ratio, ensuring exceptional legibility for high-density financial data terminals and analytical reports.

## Chapter 3: Typographic Hierarchy

### 3.1 Typeface Selection
Typographic consistency reinforces brand recognition and operational clarity. DizerEdge utilizes a dual-typeface system pairing geometric authority with extreme legibility across data-dense interfaces.

- **Primary Display & Headings:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** is utilized for all brand titles, section headers, and promotional marketing materials. It offers open apertures, distinct geometric forms, and a commanding executive presence.
- **Body Copy & Data Interfaces:** **Inter** (supplemented by **JetBrains Mono** for raw code and tabular execution data) is utilized for paragraphs, documentation, and software dashboards. Its neutral, highly optimized letterforms prevent eye fatigue during extended analytical sessions.

## Chapter 4: Logo Architecture & Usage Standards

### 4.1 Monogram & Logotype Construction
The DizerEdge logo mark represents the intersection of algorithmic mathematics and software engineering. The geometric monogram fuses the letter **"D"** with an ascending vector curve symbolizing financial optimization and market growth. The outer boundary is enclosed within a precise grid, signifying regulatory containment, risk mitigation, and security.

### 4.2 Clear Space & Minimum Sizing
To preserve visual integrity, the DizerEdge logo must always be surrounded by an unobstructed clear space equal to or greater than the height of the capital "D" in the logotype. 

- **Digital Minimum Size:** The logo mark must never be rendered at a height smaller than 24 pixels on digital displays.
- **Print Minimum Size:** In physical corporate stationery or print collateral, the logo width must not fall below 25 millimeters.

### 4.3 Prohibited Logo Modifications
To maintain brand equity and official standing, the following modifications are strictly prohibited:
- Altering the approved color ratios of the monogram or logotype.
- Applying drop shadows, gradients, or unauthorized dimensional effects to flat corporate marks.
- Placing the primary dark navy logo onto low-contrast dark backgrounds without the designated white container or inverse variant.
- Distorting, stretching, or rearranging the relative scale between the monogram and the brand typography.

The corporate header must feature the official DizerEdge logotype aligned to the top-left margin, accompanied by page numbering in the footer set in 10px Inter.

Title slides feature a solid Deep Midnight Navy background with white typography and a subtle Sovereign Gold accent bar. Content slides utilize light backgrounds with high-contrast slate headers and precise data visualization tables.
