DizerEdge Verify

Establish identity before trust is granted.

DizerEdge Bio

Bring biometrics into your verification journey.

Use face and fingerprint signals across digital interactions and compatible physical workflows.

Discuss DizerEdge Bio

Capabilities

  • Consent-based 1:1 face verification and liveness integration
  • Face enrollment and biometric template lifecycle workflows
  • Fingerprint integration using supported platform frameworks or dedicated scanners
  • Multi-factor journeys combining biometrics, device possession, PIN, or OTP

The workflow

Enroll with the appropriate controls → capture a supported signal → verify → apply policy.

Common use cases

Biometric step-up, workforce verification, and physical access.

Integration & data considerations

Hardware compatibility, lawful use, consent, retention, and biometric performance requirements must be established. Optional 1:N recognition is limited to appropriately governed, legally permitted scenarios.
